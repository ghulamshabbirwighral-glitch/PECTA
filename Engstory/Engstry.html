The Fox and the Grapes

One hot summer day, a hungry fox was walking through an orchard. He spotted a bunch of juicy, purple grapes hanging high from a vine.

The fox jumped high to reach the grapes, but he missed them. He tried again and again, running and leaping, but the grapes were simply too high.

Exhausted and frustrated, the fox finally gave up. As he walked away, he turned up his nose and muttered, "I am sure they are sour anyway. I do not want those grapes."

Moral: It is easy to despise what you cannot get.
